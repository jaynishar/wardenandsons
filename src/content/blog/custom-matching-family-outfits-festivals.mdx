## 1. Popular Occasions for Matching Family Outfits

### Festival Celebrations

Coordinating colors or patterns during major festivals makes family photo sessions extra special. Light, breathable cotton kurta-style shirts for fathers and sons, and coordinated dresses or tops for mothers and daughters are highly popular.

### Family Holidays & Shoots

Planning a family getaway to Goa or a photoshoot at Marine Drive? Custom printed family t-shirts with personalized slogans, vacation logos, or matching tropical prints are excellent choices.

### Children's Birthday Parties

Theme parties are standard today. Having the birthday child, parents, and siblings wear matching custom outfits matching the party theme (e.g., jungle safari, space, or sports) creates a cohesive, celebratory atmosphere.

## 2. Planning Your Custom Order: Key Considerations

When ordering custom garments for families, keep these three points in mind:

### Focus on Comfort (Especially for Kids)

While traditional heavy silks and heavily embroidered fabrics look beautiful, children often find them scratchy, heavy, and hot. They will quickly become fussy. At Warden & Sons, we recommend choosing **premium cotton base fabrics** and adding customization via printing, soft digital transfers, or lining the insides of embroidery with soft voile cotton.

### Account for All Ages

A family group includes members of all shapes and sizes—from newborns to grandparents. Ensure your clothing supplier can accommodate a full sizing range, from 0-3 Months bodysuits to 3XL adult sizes, in the same fabric pattern.

### Order timelines

Tailoring and custom printing take time. Organizing institutional or school athletic apparel alongside festive orders? Read our detailed guide on [School Uniform & Bulk Clothing Orders in Mumbai](/blog/school-uniform-bulk-clothing-mumbai/). For small groups (3 to 6 members), place your order at least **2 to 3 weeks** before the event. For larger events and reunions requiring bulk production, allow **4 to 6 weeks** for fabric sourcing, sizing collection, printing, and shipping.

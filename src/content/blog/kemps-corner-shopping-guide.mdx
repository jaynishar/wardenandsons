Located at the intersection of Pedder Road, Altamount Road, Breach Candy, and Malabar Hill, **Kemps Corner** is one of South Bombay's most historic, prestigious, and vibrant neighborhoods. Long before mega-malls opened up in the suburbs, Kemps Corner was the ultimate destination for luxury, fashion, and family shopping in Mumbai.

Today, Kemps Corner retains its unique charm. It combines high-end designer boutiques, iconic local institutions, and long-standing family-run shops that have served generations of South Mumbai families.

If you are planning a shopping trip to the neighborhood, here is our insider guide to the best spots for family and kids' essentials in Kemps Corner.

---

## 1. The Heritage Charm of Kemps Corner Shopping

Unlike modern shopping malls where everything feels standardized, shopping in Kemps Corner is an outdoor, boutique experience. Walking along the tree-lined avenues of South Bombay, you are surrounded by beautiful heritage buildings, historic landmarks like the flyover (Mumbai's first!), and premium shopfronts.

### Why Families Love Shopping Here:

- **Personal Connection:** Many stores in Kemps Corner are run by the second or third generation of the founding families, offering a level of customer trust and relationship that malls cannot replicate.
- **Hand-Picked Selection:** Local boutiques select their inventory specifically for the tastes of South Bombay residents.
- **Proximity to Landmarks:** Easy access to local cafes, parks (like Hanging Gardens and Priyadarshini Park), and clubs.

## 3. Grab a Bite: Best Cafes & Restaurants Nearby

Shopping can build up quite an appetite, especially when traveling with kids. Kemps Corner has some fantastic culinary institutions:

- **The Sassy Spoon / Patisserie:** A beautiful, chic spot perfect for desserts, pastries, and a light continental lunch.
- **Soam:** Located just down the road toward Chowpatty, Soam is legendary for its clean, delicious, seasonal Gujarati and traditional Indian street food. Kids love their safe, hygienic chaat and milkshakes.
- **Iconic local bakeries:** Perfect for grabbing a quick puff pastry or bread to take home.

---

## 4. Tips for Shopping in Kemps Corner

1.  **Parking Can Be Challenging:** Like most of South Bombay, street parking is limited. We recommend using a driver, taking a local taxi, or parking at nearby designated parking zones.
2.  **Combine Shopping with Playtime:** If you have young kids, make it a fun day out. Do your shopping in the morning, grab lunch, and head to **Hanging Gardens** or **Priyadarshini Park** in the evening to let the kids run around.
3.  **Use Digital Services:** If you love a shop’s products but want to skip the traffic, always check if they offer WhatsApp or home delivery services.

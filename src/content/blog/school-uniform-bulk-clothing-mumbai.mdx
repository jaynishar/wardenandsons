## Key Factors for Bulk Clothing Success in Mumbai

### 1. Fabric Selection is Crucial

Because children wear school uniforms and sports uniforms for 6 to 8 hours a day, the choice of fabric directly impacts their comfort and focus.

- **For Classrooms:** Choose cotton-rich blends. While 100% cotton is soft, adding a tiny percentage of high-grade polyester prevents excessive wrinkling and increases durability over daily wash cycles.
- **For Sports (P.E. Uniforms):** Go with **dry-fit microfiber**. It wicks sweat away from the body, drying rapidly in Mumbai's humid climate.
- **For Winterwear/School Blazers:** Light fleece or cotton-blend knits are best for Mumbai's mild climate, rather than heavy wool.

### 2. Accurate Sizing Management

Children's growth spurts are unpredictable. When ordering uniforms or event gear, always ensure your supplier provides a detailed sizing chart and a set of **physical size trial samples**. At Warden & Sons, we recommend ordering a small percentage of extra pieces in larger sizes to accommodate late admissions or sudden growth spurts.

### 3. Production & Delivery Timelines

The school academic year in Mumbai typically begins in June. Similarly, major sports tournaments happen in the winter months (November to January).

- **Plan Ahead:** You should begin discussing uniform designs and fabric sourcing at least **8 to 12 weeks** in advance.
- **Monsoon Factor:** Remember that drying and finishing processes can take slightly longer during Mumbai's heavy monsoon months (June to September). Factor this into your delivery timelines.

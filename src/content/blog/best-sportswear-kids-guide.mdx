## 1. Dry-Fit vs. Cotton: Which Sportswear Fabric is Best?

Understanding fabrics is the first step in buying kids' activewear.

### Dry-Fit (Microfiber Polyester)

Dry-fit is a high-performance, microfiber, polyester fabric that moves sweat away from the body to the outer surface of the garment, where it evaporates quickly.

- **Best For:** High-intensity sports like football, basketball, running, and tennis.
- **Advantages:** Lightweight, wrinkle-resistant, dries in minutes, and does not cling to the skin.
- **Ideal Season:** Year-round in Mumbai, but particularly crucial during the humid summer and monsoon months.

### 100% Cotton Sportswear

- **Best For:** Low-intensity activities, yoga, stretching, and post-workout cooling down.
- **Advantages:** Hypoallergenic, extremely soft, and comfortable.
- **Disadvantages:** Absorbs moisture but holds onto it, making it heavy and slow to dry.

## 2. Sports-Specific Gear Checklist

### Cricket Whites (Essential for Local Academies)

If your child is enrolled in a cricket coaching camp in Mumbai, you will need a few sets of cricket whites.

- **The Fit:** Cricket matches can last for hours under the sun. Pants must be loose-fitting around the thighs to allow comfortable running and bowling strides.
- **Reinforced Knees:** Look for pants with reinforced stitching or double layering at the knees to withstand sliding and fielding stops.
- **Breathability:** Opt for premium poly-cotton blends or light dry-fit whites that keep them cool during long sessions.

### Football Jerseys & Sport Sets

Kids love supporting their favorite clubs. Football jerseys should be made of durable mesh or structured dry-fit fabric to handle rough tackles and pulls. Look for matching quad-sets (t-shirt and shorts) that offer complete freedom of movement.

### Swimwear & Goggles

Whether they are swimming at the Cricket Club of India (CCI), Bombay Gymkhana, or local school pools, ensure their swimwear offers **UV protection** and is chlorine-resistant. Don't forget comfortable, leak-proof goggles with anti-fog lenses for safety and visibility.

---

## Sportswear Sizing Tips for Parents

1.  **Don't Buy Too Large:** While buying a size up in casual wear is a common parental practice to save money, sportswear should fit relatively close to the body. Loose fabric can trip children, snag on equipment, or chaff the skin.
2.  **Check the Waistband:** Ensure elastic waistbands are soft and feature an internal drawstring so shorts can be adjusted securely.
3.  **Flatlock Seams:** Look for activewear constructed with flatlock seams (flat stitching). This prevents the seams from rubbing against the skin, avoiding chafing.

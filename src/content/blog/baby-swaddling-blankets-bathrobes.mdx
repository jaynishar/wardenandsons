For centuries, parents across the globe have practiced **swaddling**—the art of snugly wrapping a newborn baby in a light blanket. Swaddling helps newborns transition from the warm, tight environment of the womb to the outside world. It prevents the Moro reflex (the sudden startle reflex that causes babies to throw their arms out) from waking them, leading to longer, more peaceful sleep.

However, swaddling a baby in a coastal city like Mumbai presents a unique challenge: **overheating**. With high humidity and temperatures, using a heavy or synthetic blanket for swaddling can cause heat rash, discomfort, and in worst cases, increase the risk of SIDS (Sudden Infant Death Syndrome).

## The Essentials of Safe Swaddling in Mumbai

When swaddling your baby in Mumbai's climate, follow these critical safety guidelines:

### 1. The Right Fabric is Everything

Never use thick fleece, heavy cotton knits, or synthetic fabrics for swaddling. Instead, use:

- **100% Muslin Cotton:** This is the gold standard. Muslin is a lightweight, open-weave fabric that allows heat to escape and air to circulate, keeping the baby perfectly cool. Pairing your muslin swaddle with breathable base layers is essential; consult our checklist on [Newborn Clothing Essentials in Mumbai](/blog/newborn-clothing-essentials-mumbai/) for hospital bag planning.
- **Organic Cotton Knits:** Stretchy and soft, allowing for a snug swaddle that still lets the baby move their limbs slightly.

### 2. Monitor Room Temperature

If your baby is swaddled, ensure the room is kept at a comfortable temperature. If you use an air conditioner, set it between 22°C to 24°C. If the room is warm, a single layer of muslin over a simple diaper is enough.

### 3. Check for Overheating

Always check your baby's neck and chest. If they feel hot, damp with sweat, or have red cheeks, unwrap them immediately.

## Swaddles vs. Baby Blankets vs. Bathrobes

New parents are often confused by the different terminology. Here is what each item is for:

### Muslin Swaddle Wraps (0-3 Months)

These are large, square sheets (usually around 47" x 47"). They are designed specifically for swaddling. Because they are large, they are easy to tuck in securely.

- **Pro Tip:** Once your baby learns to roll over (typically around 3-4 months), you must stop swaddling their arms to keep them safe.

### Receiving & Baby Blankets (0-12 Months)

Unlike swaddles, receiving blankets are typically smaller and made of slightly thicker woven cotton or flannel. They are used for laying on the floor for tummy time, draping over a car seat, or keeping the baby cozy in an air-conditioned room.

### Hooded Towels & Bathrobes (0-24 Months)

Bath time is a special bonding routine. A newborn baby cools down rapidly when wet.

- **Hooded Towels:** Essential for newborns. The hood keeps their wet head warm while you dry their body.
- **Bathrobes:** Ideal as they grow into toddlers (12-24 months). A soft cotton bathrobe is easy to slip on after a bath, pool session, or beach visit.

## Swaddling Step-by-Step

> 1. Fold the top corner of the muslin blanket down. Lay your baby face up on the blanket, with their head above the folded corner.
> 2. Pull the baby's left arm down. Take the left side of the blanket, pull it snug across the chest, and tuck it securely under the right side.
> 3. Fold the bottom of the blanket up over the feet, ensuring their hips can move freely (do not pull the legs straight or tight).
> 4. Pull the right arm down. Wrap the right side of the blanket across the chest and tuck it under the left side.

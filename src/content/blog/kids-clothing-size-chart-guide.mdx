## 3. Why Sizes Vary by Brand and Fabric

Clothing sizes vary across manufacturers. A size 26 from one maker often fits differently from another maker's size 26.

Key reasons why sizing varies include:

1. **Indian Numbered Sizing vs Age Labels:** Indian kids' wear uses chest or length numbers (20 to 36). An Indian size 24 generally matches an international 4–5 Years, but garment cuts differ between mills.
2. **Woven Cotton vs Knit Hosiery:** 100% woven cotton poplin and linen have no stretch, requiring room for movement. Combed cotton knits stretch naturally and fit flexibly.
3. **Sports Blends:** School sportswear and cricket whites use dryfit polyester or poly-cotton blends. These fabrics resist shrinkage but have less stretch than cotton knits.
4. **Natural Fabric Washing:** Pure cotton and muslin relax slightly after washing. We factor this in when assisting parents with sizing.

## 4. How to Measure Your Child at Home

Using a soft tailor's tape takes three minutes and prevents exchange visits:

1. **Chest:** Wrap tape around the fullest chest area, under arms, keeping it level without pulling tight.
2. **Waist:** Measure around the natural waistline, just above the hips, while breathing comfortably.
3. **Hips:** Wrap tape around the widest seat area for trousers, shorts, and skirts.
4. **Height:** Have your child stand barefoot against a wall. Mark head top and measure straight down.
5. **Flat-Garment Method:** If your child dislikes standing still, lay a well-fitting garment flat. Measure pit-to-pit for chest width, and shoulder-to-hem for garment length.

## 5. Practical Sizing Advice for Parents

Keep these four practical tips in mind when choosing sizes:

1. **When in Doubt, Size Up:** If between sizes, pick the larger one. A roomy garment soon fits well, while a tight one cannot be worn.
2. **Account for Mumbai Weather:** Tight clothes cause discomfort in tropical warmth. Loose-fitting cotton and muslin allow air circulation and keep kids comfortable.
3. **Look for Adjustable Features:** Seek out elastic waistbands, functional drawstrings, buttonhole tabs, and roll-up cuffs that adapt as children grow.
4. **Select by Purpose:** Buy nightwear roomier for sleeping comfort. Ensure uniforms allow free shoulder movement for carrying schoolbags.

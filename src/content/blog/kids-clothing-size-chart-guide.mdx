## 1. General Sizing Categories: The Basics

Children's clothing is typically grouped into four main age ranges:

- **Baby (0 - 24 Months):** Sized by months (e.g., 0-3M, 3-6M, 6-12M, 12-18M, 18-24M). Sizing is based primarily on weight and height. Shopping for an infant? Browse our [Newborn & Infant Clothes (0-24 Months)](/shop/newborn/) designed with extra diaper rise.
- **Toddler (2 - 4 Years):** Sized by years. Designed with extra room in the diaper area for younger toddlers.
- **Little Kids (4 - 7 Years):** Sized by height and chest.
- **Big Kids / Youth (8 - 16 Years):** Sized by height, chest, and waist. Fits become more structured. Explore our [Boys Playwear & Clothes](/shop/boys/) and [Girls Dresses & Sleepwear](/shop/girls/) collections.

## 3. Sizing Hacks for Busy Parents

To avoid returns and exchange trips, keep these three expert tips in mind:

### The "Always Size Up" Rule

If your child falls exactly between two sizes, or if you are buying seasonal clothes like winterwear or nightwear, **always choose the larger size**. Children grow fast. A garment that is slightly loose will fit them perfectly in a few weeks, whereas a tight garment cannot be worn at all.

### Check the Fabric Composition

- **100% Cotton & Muslin:** Natural fabrics can shrink slightly (about 3-5%) during the first few washes. To understand how different cotton weaves behave over time, compare options in our [Boys vs Girls Nightwear Fabrics Guide](/blog/boys-vs-girls-nightwear-fabrics/).
- **Hosiery & Dryfit:** These fabrics are stretchy and flexible, meaning they are more forgiving in terms of fit.

### Look for Adjustability

Search for clothing details that allow the garment to grow with your child:

- Pants with internal elastic drawstrings.
- Dresses with loose, A-line shapes that can transition into tunics as she gets taller.
- Stretchy ribbed cuffs on hoodies that can be folded up.

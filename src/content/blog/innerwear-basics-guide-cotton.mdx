## What is GSM in Cotton Fabrics?

GSM stands for **Grams per Square Meter**. It is a standard metric used to measure the weight, thickness, and density of a fabric.

Understanding GSM helps you select the right garment:

- **Low GSM (100 - 130 GSM):** Extremely thin, lightweight fabric. For newborn babies with sensitive skin, lighter weights and open-weave muslins are essential; read our guide on [Newborn Clothing Essentials in Mumbai](/blog/newborn-clothing-essentials-mumbai/).
- **Medium GSM (140 - 160 GSM):** Standard weight for average t-shirts. Offers a balance of breathability and durability.
- **Premium Medium Weight (170 - 190 GSM):** Often referred to as **180 GSM Cotton**. This is the sweet spot for premium, high-quality t-shirts, lounge shorts, and underwear. It is thick enough to be durable and opaque, yet breathable and soft.
- **Heavy GSM (200+ GSM):** Heavyweight cotton. Used for heavy hoodies, winter sweatshirts, and structured streetwear. Too warm for Mumbai’s general weather.

---

## Why 180 GSM Cotton is the Ideal Choice for Mumbai Basics

For daily wear t-shirts and lounge basics in Mumbai, **180 GSM cotton** is highly recommended:

1.  **Superior Sweat Absorption:** Thick, natural cotton fibers absorb moisture rapidly, keeping you dry and preventing sweat patches.
2.  **Long-Term Durability:** Basics undergo frequent washing. 180 GSM cotton resists shrinking, maintains its structure, and stays soft even after dozens of machine washes.
3.  **Perfect Draping & Opacity:** It falls cleanly on the body without clinging, and it is completely opaque, making it suitable for step-out errands or casual loungewear.

# Warden & Sons — Catalogue Photography Shotlist

**Setup & Instructions:**
- **Target Count:** About 35–40 photos (one signature photo per sub-category).
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 portrait (minimum 1600 × 2000 px).
- **Background:** Plain off-white wall, neutral linen bedsheet, or clean wooden floor.
- **Lighting:** Soft natural window light from one side (no direct camera flash or yellow bulbs).
- **Camera:** Smartphone mounted steadily on a tripod. Keep the exact same camera height, distance, and crop for every shot in a category.
- **Save Location:** Save original high-resolution captures in `assets/photos/<category>/<sub>/1.jpg`.
